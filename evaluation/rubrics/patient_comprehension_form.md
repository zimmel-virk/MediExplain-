# Patient Comprehension Test — Form

**For the researcher administering the test**

This form is administered verbally to each patient participant after they have
read (or listened to) one AI-generated, doctor-approved patient summary.

## Comprehension questions

Read each question to the patient in their preferred language. Mark their
answer below. Each question has one correct answer (✓) and three distractors.

### Q1. What was the main problem the doctor said you have?

Read out four options, randomising order:
1. [Correct: insert from consultation, e.g. "Throat infection"]
2. [Distractor 1: insert plausible alternative]
3. [Distractor 2]
4. "I don't remember"

Answer chosen: ____  Correct? [ ] Yes  [ ] No

## After collection

Tabulate all responses in a spreadsheet with columns:
`patient_code, language, format, q1_correct, q2_correct, ... q5_correct,
total_correct, confidence_1, confidence_2, confidence_3, format_preference`

Report:
- Overall mean correctness across all patients (target: ≥ 80%)
- Per-language mean correctness (any language < 60% needs investigation)
- Mean confidence scores
- Format preference distribution
- Themes from open feedback (group similar comments)
